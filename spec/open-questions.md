### A-02 -> REQ-04, REQ-05, REQ-13, DEC-11, DEC-12
**Question:** What's the one thing you'd warn a new maintainer about before they touch this?
**Answer (2026-06-12, Project maintainer):** There are several variants of this project. Submission files are annual, based on official end-of-year 15.2 reports, and standardized across all LEAs. Request files are ad-hoc: usually subsets of 15.2 students, sometimes newly identified students, and in some cases non-graduates. The processes are close but produce different upload outputs, especially headers and leading row semantics. NSC downloads do not identify their originating variant, so matching returned reports to request files versus annual school/district outcomes requires inspecting the results and inferring from counts and cohort size.
